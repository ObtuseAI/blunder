## Product decision

The seven Fractal Sovereign epochs are implemented and operationalized as the authority-closed CCOS substrate. The historical implementation plan remains an evidence record; it is not the active backlog.

Blunder vNext keeps the Python intelligence core, constitutional enforcement, isolated missions, deterministic verification, durable CCOS evidence, and browser recovery shell. The active program adds a versioned local service, a native desktop host, automatic objective orchestration, an Engineering Graph, typed agents, environment management, layered memory, and evidence-gated improvement.

The product remains a `CCOS_RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE`. Promotion is autonomous only through the immutable threshold policies and append-only decision ledger. Provider use, federation, external deployment, credential expansion, live source self-modification, and recursive-improvement claims remain disabled unless their separate authority and evidence gates pass.

## Permanent boundaries

- Missing identity, policy, provenance, evidence, rollback, budget, or approval fails closed.
- Prompts, acknowledgements, consensus, fitness, memory, and imported data never create authority.
- The renderer receives no raw credential, unrestricted filesystem access, shell access, or direct provider capability.
- Every promotion is evaluated autonomously against a content-identified constitutional policy. There is no manual promotion bypass and thresholds cannot be lowered by a task.
- Promotion activates only its governed local projection. Source integration, credentials, authority expansion, release signing, and external deployment remain separate effects and never follow implicitly from promotion.
- Synthetic runs may falsify a candidate but cannot prove measured user or longitudinal improvement.
- Every milestone retains the native suite, private proof, secret scan, source identity, and shared-folder handoff evidence.

## Implemented v1 control interfaces

- `PromotionPolicyV1` is code-owned and content-identified for capability, memory, genome, organ, agent-team, research-concept, strategy, improvement, and release-candidate scopes. Request payloads cannot supply or weaken thresholds.
- `PromotionEvidenceV1` binds the candidate and evaluation identities, evidence digests, exact constitutional hard gates, independence, holdout, uncertainty, cost, contamination, contradiction, rollback, provenance, authority, budget, source, and reproducibility observations.
- `PromotionDecisionV1` records every passed and failed gate. Only `PROMOTED_AUTONOMOUS_THRESHOLD_PASS` promotes; improvement, genome, and organ failures preserve exact `NO_CHAMPION`.
- `GET /api/v1/promotions` returns policies and the tamper-evident decision ledger. `POST /api/v1/promotions/evaluate` evaluates and atomically records one strict evidence contract.
- `POST /api/v1/tasks/{id}/launch`, `cancel`, and `integrate` bind tasks to the existing isolated mission runtime. Provider authority comes only from the standing project policy; integration still requires exact `INTEGRATE {task-id}` and applies only to the uncommitted review tree.
